Behind the estimate

One dataset.
A transparent pipeline.

The demonstration recreates the notebook's Gradient Boosting workflow. It retrains from the provided Ames data on first prediction and reuses that fitted pipeline while the app is running.

01

Prepare the data

Median imputation for numeric columns; most-frequent imputation and one-hot encoding for categorical columns.

02

Fit the model

Gradient Boosting with 200 estimators, a 0.05 learning rate, depth 3, and random state 42.

03

Read the evidence

The notebook's 80/20 split reserves 292 homes for validation. Overall quality and above-grade living area are its strongest reported features.

What the notebook found
